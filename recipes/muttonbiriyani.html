Mutton Biriyani

Description:

Mutton Biriyani is a flavorful and aromatic rice dish made with tender mutton pieces and fragrant spices.

Ingredients:

  • 200g mutton, cut into pieces
  • 1 cup basmati rice
  • 2 tbsp cooking oil
  • 1 large onion, sliced
  • 2 tomatoes, chopped
  • 1 tsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds
  • 1 tsp coriander powder
  • 1 tsp red chili powder
  • 1 tsp turmeric powder
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • Salt to taste

Steps:

  1. Rinse the rice and set aside.
  2. In a pot, heat oil and add cumin seeds.
  3. Add sliced onions and sauté until golden brown.
  4. Add tomatoes and cook until they soften.
  5. Add ginger-garlic paste and cook for a minute.
  6. Add mutton pieces and cook until they change color.
  7. Add all the spices and salt. Mix well.
  8. Add water and bring to a boil.
  9. Reduce heat and simmer for 20 minutes or until mutton is tender.
  10. Add the cooked rice and mix gently.
  11. Cover and cook for another 10 minutes.
