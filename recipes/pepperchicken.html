Pepper Chicken

Description:

Pepper chicken is a hot, spicy, and fragrant dish, predominantly from South India (often called Chicken Milagu Varuval), featuring chicken cooked with generous amounts of freshly ground black pepper, cumin, fennel, and curry leaves. It is typically served as a dry or semi-dry stir-fry, making it an excellent starter, appetizer, or side dish for roti, naan, or rice

Ingredients:

  • 500 grams chicken
  • Oil
    • Cumin Seeds
  • Small Onions
  • Coriander Seeds
  • Fennel Seeds
  • Black Pepper
  • Salt
  • Ginger Garlic Paste
  • Curry Leaves
  • Cinnamon
  • Cloves
  • Cardamom
  • Red Chilli Powder
  • Turmeric Powder
  • Garam Masala Powder

Steps:

  1. To a fry pan add 1 teaspoon fennel seeds, 1 teaspoon whole pepper and ½ teaspoon coriander seeds.
  2. Dry roast until golden and aromatic.
  3. Transfer to a plate cool down completely.
  4. Grind it to a powder slightly coarse is also ok. Set aside.
  5. Heat 2 tablespoon oil in a kadai. Add one 1 inch cinnamon, 2 cloves, 2 cardamom, ½ teaspoon cumin seeds. Let it splutter
  6. Add few curry leaves let it splutter.Give a quick saute.
  7. Add ½ cup heaped small onion sliced along with required salt.
  8. Saute until onions turn transparent then add 1 teaspoon ginger garlic paste.
  9. Add 300 gms cleaned chicken pieces along with ¼ teaspoon turmeric powder.
  10. Saute well for at least 5 minutes until the texture and color of chicken changes.
  11. Add ¾ cup water and give a quick mix.Let it start to boil.
  12. Cook covered for 20-25 mins or until chicken tends tender.
  13. Add fresh prepared powder along with ¼ teaspoon red chilli powder and ¼ teaspoon garam masala powder.Saute for 2 mins.
  14. Finally add ¼ teaspoon freshly grind pepper powder along with few fresh curry leaves.
  15. Give a quick mix and switch off.Pepper Chicken is ready!
