Chicken Alfredo Pasta

Description:

Chicken Alfredo Pasta is a creamy and delicious dish made with fettuccine pasta, tender chicken pieces, and a rich Alfredo sauce.

Ingredients:

  • 200g fettuccine pasta
  • 200g chicken breast, diced
  • 100ml heavy cream
  • 50g butter
  • 100g grated Parmesan cheese
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Steps:

  1. Cook the fettuccine pasta according to package instructions.
  2. In a large skillet, melt the butter and sauté the garlic until fragrant.
  3. Add the chicken pieces and cook until they are no longer pink.
  4. Pour in the heavy cream and bring to a gentle boil.
  5. Stir in the Parmesan cheese until fully incorporated.
  6. Add the cooked pasta to the sauce and toss to coat.
  7. Season with salt and pepper to taste.
